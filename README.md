# Xtreme Carpet Cleaning — concept site (demo #10)

Spec demo for X-treme Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning, El Paso. Built on the `greenlife-solutions-demo` layout
(centered hero with tilted floating photos, colored service cards with round photos, marquee strip, carousel,
timeline steps) with a blue accent from their logo and equipment. No build step.

## Hook
Their listed website (xtremecarpetcleaningept.com) returns 404. Facebook is the only working presence
(facebook.com/xtremecarpetcleaningept, ~1K followers, 94% recommend / 25 reviews).

## Facts used (all from their Facebook page)
- Family owned and operated since 2000; certified, licensed and insured
- Phone (915) 595-0581, xtremecleanup@gmail.com, Bob Mitchell Dr, El Paso (no street number published)
- Services: carpet and upholstery cleaning, air duct cleaning, flood / water damage restoration
- Proud sponsors of the Southwest Prospects basketball team
